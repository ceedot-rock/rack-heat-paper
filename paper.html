Adiabatic

Ordinary CMOS charges a node and throws the charge away. Adiabatic style ramps a recoverable clock slowly versus RC so most of that energy can return to the rail. Benefits: less switching heat on the gates you actually build this way. Costs: time, clock-generator loss, leakage, incomplete recovery. Control plane only.
